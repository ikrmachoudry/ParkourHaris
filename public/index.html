How to play

  • WASD / arrows – run (relative to the camera)
  • SPACE – jump (hold for a higher jump)
  • SHIFT or F – dive forward (great for long gaps)
  • MOUSE or Q/E – turn the camera • M – mute

Reach the FINISH gate – there is no time limit, take your time. Spinning bars, hammers and rolling balls knock you back – fall into the water and you restart at the last checkpoint flag.
