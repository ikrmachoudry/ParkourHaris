Haris is 10 years old and loves making games with AI. He has the ideas, tests every build and keeps asking “how does that work?” – and he wants to learn more AI and more of the concepts behind fun games.

WHAT HARIS BUILDS

  • PUNJABI GTA – a big open city with cars, a jeep, missions and a mountain.
  • JANG – a battle-royale: parachute in, loot, shoot, survive.
  • New ideas every week – he plays, finds what is not fun yet, and asks for it to be better.

HOW A GAME GETS MADE

  • 1. Idea – what is the fun? (a jeep you can drive with friends)
  • 2. Describe it – explain it clearly to the AI, step by step.
  • 3. Build & test – play it, break it, note what feels wrong.
  • 4. Improve – fix, repeat, share with friends.

SKILLS A GAME MAKER GROWS

  • Creativity – stories, worlds, characters.
  • Clear thinking – asking the AI the right question.
  • Maths & logic – speed, jumps, scores, maps.
  • Coding basics – reading a little code makes you a better director.
  • Art & sound – colours, 3D models, music.
  • Testing & patience – every pro game is fixed many times.

WHERE THIS CAN LEAD

  • Game designer • game programmer • 3D artist • level designer
  • AI / machine-learning engineer • tools builder
  • Starter paths: Scratch, Roblox Studio, Godot, Unity – and keep using AI as a helper, not a shortcut: always ask it why.

How to play

ON FOOT

  • WASD / arrows – walk (hold SHIFT to run)
  • SPACE – jump
  • MOUSE – look around (click the game first) • Q/E turn the camera
  • Z – crouch / stand
  • F – punch
  • G – grab the swing / hold on

WEAPONS

  • V – switch weapon (fist, bat, pistol, AK-47 – only the ones you own)
  • CLICK – fire the gun / swing the bat (hold for the AK-47)
  • B – swing the bat
  • R – aim (over the shoulder). Aim low to shoot tyres, higher for windows
  • The gun reloads by itself when the magazine is empty. Ammo and guns are bought in the weapon store.

CARS

  • C – get in the nearest car / get out. Stand next to a traffic car to take it.
  • W gas • S brake and reverse • AD steer
  • SPACE – handbrake (slides the car round corners)
  • H – horn • L – headlights
  • Drive a stolen car onto the ring at INTELLIMUM AUTOS and stop: they pay cash, less if it is smashed. Park in the repair bay to fix your car.

MAP, MONEY, MISSIONS

  • M or TAB – big map • the small map shows where you are
  • Step on the pad inside the weapon store to buy guns and ammo
  • Step on the pad in the mission lobby to pick STUNT JUMP or THIEF CHASE. Missions pay cash
  • LEFT CLICK – fire / punch • hold RIGHT CLICK – aim
  • ESC or P – menu (volume, fullscreen, restart)

On a phone: left thumb = joystick (walk, or steer and gas in a car). Round buttons on the right: fist = punch, arrows = jump, gun = change weapon, crosshair = aim, crouch. FIRE shoots, BAT swings. The car button gets in and out; in a car use the arrows, pedals, handbrake, lights and horn buttons. MENU is top right.
